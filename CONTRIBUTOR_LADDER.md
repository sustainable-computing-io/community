# Kepler Contributor Ladder

Kepler is an open source project built by and for its community.
We encourage contributors to grow within the project and take on
additional responsibilities based on their interests, experience,
and availability.

This document describes contributor roles, responsibilities, and
opportunities for progression within the Kepler community.

## Contributor Roles

| Role | Responsibilities | Expected Experience |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Contributor | Contribute to the project and community | Open to everyone |
| Reviewer | Review contributions and support project quality | Consistent contributions and demonstrated expertise |
| Maintainer | Support project direction, development, and long-term health | Sustained contributions, ownership, and sound judgment |

## Contributor

Anyone who contributes to Kepler is considered a contributor.

Contributions can be technical or non-technical, including code,
documentation, testing, community engagement, education, and
user support.

### Expectations

- Follow the project's contribution guidelines.
- Follow the CNCF Code of Conduct.
- Participate constructively in project discussions.
- Collaborate respectfully with other contributors.

### Growing as a Contributor

Contributors interested in taking on additional responsibilities
are encouraged to:

- Participate regularly in project activities.
- Develop expertise in one or more areas of the project.
- Provide constructive feedback on contributions.
- Help address issues and support other contributors.
- Participate in community meetings and discussions.

See the [Contributing Guide](CONTRIBUTING.md) for information
on how to get involved.

## Reviewer

Reviewers are experienced contributors who help ensure the
quality and consistency of contributions within their areas
of expertise.

### Expected Experience

- A history of meaningful contributions to the project.
- Familiarity with relevant project components and practices.
- Demonstrated ability to provide constructive feedback.
- Active collaboration with contributors and maintainers.

### Responsibilities

- Review contributions within their areas of expertise.
- Provide timely and constructive feedback.
- Help contributors follow project standards and best practices.
- Support issue triage and technical discussions.
- Help mentor and onboard new contributors.

### Becoming a Reviewer

Contributors interested in becoming reviewers should discuss
their interest with existing maintainers.

Relevant experience, contribution history, collaboration,
and demonstrated expertise should be considered when discussing
readiness for the role.

## Maintainer

Maintainers take responsibility for the long-term health,
development, and sustainability of the Kepler project.

Maintainers may focus on different areas, including technical
development, release management, documentation, community
engagement, and contributor experience.

### Expected Experience

- Sustained and meaningful contributions to the project.
- Demonstrated ownership and responsibility.
- A strong understanding of relevant project areas.
- Sound judgment and constructive collaboration.
- Commitment to the long-term success of the project.

### Responsibilities

- Guide project development and priorities.
- Support the quality, reliability, and sustainability
  of the project.
- Help coordinate project activities and initiatives.
- Support and mentor contributors and reviewers.
- Foster a welcoming and inclusive community.
- Collaborate with other maintainers on project decisions.

### Becoming a Maintainer

Contributors interested in becoming maintainers should discuss
their interest with the existing maintainers.

Readiness for this role should reflect sustained contributions,
demonstrated responsibility, collaboration, and commitment
to the project.

The current Kepler maintainers are listed in
[MAINTAINERS.md](https://github.com/sustainable-computing-io/kepler/blob/main/MAINTAINERS.md).

## Contributor Progression

The contributor ladder is intended to provide guidance and
encourage professional and community growth.

Progression is not based solely on the number of pull requests,
commits, or lines of code contributed. The quality, consistency,
and impact of contributions are equally important.

Both technical and non-technical contributions should be
recognized throughout the contributor journey.

Not every contributor needs to progress through every role.
Contributors are encouraged to participate in ways that align
with their interests, expertise, and availability.

This document does not introduce new nomination procedures,
approval processes, or repository permissions. Existing project
processes continue to apply.
