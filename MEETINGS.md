# Community Meetings

Kepler holds twice-monthly community meetings. Everyone is welcome, no invitation needed.

## Schedule

- [Meeting calendar](https://zoom-lfx.platform.linuxfoundation.org/meetings/kepler?view=month)
- iCal feed, for Google, Apple, Outlook, and other calendars:

  ```text
  https://webcal.prod.itx.linuxfoundation.org/lfx/a092M00001MtBxZQAV
  ```

Recordings are available from each calendar event.

## Agenda and Notes

Add topics to the agenda and read past notes in the
[meeting notes doc](https://docs.google.com/document/d/1YtflOiC8Eq61MIrX2DW2KT2XcJjBZnvgyKrkjUCr_ZE/edit?usp=sharing).

## Between Meetings

Ask questions in [`#kepler-project`](https://cloud-native.slack.com/archives/C05QK3KN3HT)
on [CNCF Slack](https://slack.cncf.io/).
